**Claude** · 2026-10-22 07:16

B61 is fixed, and the app has been restarted. Press **Analyse** on the project to see it.

**Why it happened:** in the drawing, B61's two faces stop about 400 mm below BC6 and then bend as two curves into BC6. The app reads straight beams and joins them to their supports *before* it reads curves. So when it reached B61, nothing seemed to hold its top end, and it carried B61 straight on up to BC6 through the corner. The curve was then added next to it. That gave a straight beam plus a curved piece side by side, instead of one beam that bends.

**The fix:** once the curves are read, the app checks for a straight beam whose drawn faces end exactly where a curve of the same width starts and carries on in the same direction. That beam now stops where the curve begins, and the curve carries it on into BC6. The empty corner beside the curve now becomes part of the slab next to it, as drawn.

**Checked on every plan on this PC:**
