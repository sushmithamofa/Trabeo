**What was wrong:** your drawing draws each wide girder (GB1 to GB14, 700 to 1100 wide) as two lines, one for each face. Its size label, like "GB1(1100X1200)", sits on the girder layer. The app has a fixed limit: it never joins two lines more than 700 apart into one beam. So for every girder:
- the girder was centred on one face line, so its band stuck out half its width past where it really is;
- the other face line became a separate unmarked 230 beam: the yellow band beside each girder;
