**Claude** · 2026-10-17 06:51

**Short answer:** replace the old drawing with the revision. Don't keep both in Import.

## Why not keep both
If the old and revised first-floor drawings are both in Import, the app treats them as two separate plans and builds both. You would get doubled beams and columns on that floor. Each floor should have exactly one current drawing.

## What to do today
1. In **Import CAD**, click **×** next to the old first-floor file. This only takes it off the list: the file stays on your PC and earlier results are kept.
2. Add the revised file.
3. Press **Analyse** again.

## Problems with doing it that way today
- **Some decisions won't carry over.**
  - Floor levels you entered carry over.
  - Decisions you applied to every element with a mark (e.g. "all B12") carry over.
  - Decisions on a single element don't carry over. The app records those against the file name and the element's number in that file, so a revised file with a new name doesn't match them. You would have to make those decisions again.
- **You can't see what the consultant changed.** You would have to compare the two drawings by eye.

## How I'd fix this, together with the comments feature
**1. A "Replace with revision" button on each file in Import.** The old file is kept as "previous revision". It's used only for comparison and is never built. After Analyse:
- **Your decisions and notes move to the new drawing.** They're matched by mark, floor and position. Anything that can't be matched is listed for you to check, not silently dropped.
- **You get a "What changed in this revision" list:** beams added or removed, sizes changed, moved elements. Each one can be clicked to see it in Review.
- **Each note shows its result:** resolved in the revision, still the same, or element removed.

**2. A "Load consultant's reply" button for the returned Excel.**
- Reading their file:
  - The app matches each reply to its note by the row number.
  - The reply shows in that element's panel in Review.
- If the reply gives a value, e.g. "B47 = 230×450", the app offers it as a choice: **"Use consultant's value"**. You click to accept, and it's saved as a decision noted "from consultant reply, 17 Oct". It's never applied by itself, in keeping with your rule that nothing is assumed.
- Notes the consultant answered are marked answered. You close them once you're happy.

**3. Normal cycle:**
1. Comment and screenshot in Review.
2. Send the Excel or PDF.
3. The consultant replies in Excel and/or sends a revised CAD.
4. Load the reply and/or replace with the revision, then Analyse.
5. Check the changes and the open notes.
6. Export to Revit.

If the consultant sends both a reply and a revised drawing, **the revised drawing wins**. The Excel reply is only used for things the drawing still doesn't show.

Say "go" to build all three parts (comments + screenshots + report, revision replace, reply import). Or "go 1" to build only the comments and report first, and add the revision and reply parts after you've tried it.
