**Claude** · 2026-10-12 09:55

You were right: the CAD reading was at fault. The depths are in the table, and the app wasn't reading most of the rows. This is now fixed, and nothing that already worked has changed.

**What went wrong.** In your "SCHEDULE OF PLNTH BEAMS", each row's beam marks run over several lines, like this:

```
PB-1,2,3,4,5,6,7,10,12,16,      230  450 ...
   17,20,30,33,36,38,39,
   ...
   77,82,83,100,101,104
```

The table reader had two faults, and neither one is specific to this consultant:
1. **It dropped long mark lists.** It decided which column a text belongs to by the text's middle. A long list like "1,2,3,…,16," starts under BEAM NOS, but its middle sits over SIZE B. So three whole rows (PB1…, PB8…, PB19…) were never read.
2. **It ignored the lines that continue a list.** A line such as "77,82,83,100,101,104" has no "PB" in front, so it was skipped. PB77 is on one of those lines.

**The fix** is in [tables.py](../../../../Documents/Structure%20CAD%20to%20Revit/New%20version/building/readers/tables.py):
- A mark list now belongs to the column it starts in.
- A line of plain numbers directly under a list ending in a comma continues that row, using the row's prefix. A list that doesn't end in a comma takes nothing from the line below.
