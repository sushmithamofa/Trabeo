**Claude** · 2026-10-27 05:24

Short answer: **beams go on the same floor, and columns go DOWN from that floor to the level below.** Columns do not go up to the floor above.

**How it works** ([build_model.py:336-346](revit/build_model.py#L336-L346), [set_column_span](revit/build_model.py#L552-L577))

The app treats every plan you give it as a **framing plan for that floor**:

- **Beams:** placed on the level you pick, with their **tops at that level**, hanging below it.
- **Normal columns (C marks):** they hold that floor up, so they **start at the level below and stop at the chosen level**, where the beams frame into them.
- **Floating columns (FC marks):** these are the exception. They **start on the girder at this level and go up** to the level above. But if a column already exists below in the same spot (from an earlier build of the floor below), the app treats the FC like a normal column and builds it from below ([build_model.py:2546-2551](revit/build_model.py#L2546-L2551)).

**For a ground floor plan specifically**

- If the Revit model **has a level below Ground** (Plinth, Foundation, Basement), the columns run from that level up to Ground.
- If **Ground is the lowest level**, there's nothing below. The app then gives the columns a fixed height of **3000 mm hanging below Ground** (`STOREY_HEIGHT_MM`). So you'd get columns going 3 m below ground level, which is probably not what you want.

**Is that right for your drawing?** It depends on what the "ground floor" sheet shows:

- If it's a **structural framing plan** (beams at ground/plinth level, with columns from the foundation up), this behaviour is correct. Add a Foundation or Plinth level in Revit first so the columns stop there and not at a made-up 3 m.
- If it's an **architectural-style plan**, where the columns drawn are the ones rising from ground to first floor, the app puts them on the wrong side. Columns go below Ground, and the beams sit at Ground, not at first floor.

So for a ground floor sheet, check which of these it is. If it's the first kind, make sure the model has a level below Ground. I haven't changed anything. If you want the column direction to be selectable per plan, I can look at that.
