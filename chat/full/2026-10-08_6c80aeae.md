**What was wrong**
1. **3D columns were turned 90°.** The drawing gives each column's angle as the direction of its long side, but the 3D view put the short side (230) along that angle. Every column ended up standing across its beam like a fin. The plan outlines and the data going to Revit were already correct.
2. **The whole drawing is 2° off square.** This DWG is drawn at about 2°/92°, and both views showed it exactly as drawn, so everything looked slightly tilted.

**What I changed**
- In both 3D views ([review.py](../../../../Documents/Structure%20CAD%20to%20Revit/New%20version/building/review.py) and [viewer3d.py](../../../../Documents/Structure%20CAD%20to%20Revit/New%20version/building/viewer3d.py)), columns now put their long side along their angle. Footings are built the same way as before.
- The plan and 3D views now find the angle most of the beam length runs at and turn the display square by that amount. If no single angle clearly dominates (under 40% of beam length), nothing is turned. This changes only what's on screen; coordinates and exports keep the drawing's real angle.
- The 3D view now opens looking from the same side as the plan, so the two match.
