# CAD to Revit: team guide

A short guide for team members: what the app does, how to sign in and set a password,
and the four steps from drawings to model. It covers only what a team member sees and
does in the browser. Nothing about running or managing the app is in it.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `CAD-to-Revit-Team-Guide.html` | The guide as 15 slides. Double-click to open it in Chrome or Edge. Works offline. |
| `CAD-to-Revit-Team-Guide.pdf` | The same 15 slides as a PDF, for e-mail or Teams. |

**Presenting:** open the HTML, press F11 for full screen, and use ← → or click to move
between slides. Moving the mouse shows small ‹ › buttons at the bottom right.
Ctrl+P saves a fresh PDF.

**The app link in the guide** is `http://DESKTOP-1B6U4I7:8770/`, with
`http://192.168.1.36:8770/` given as the fallback. If this PC's name or network
address changes, update slides 4 and 15 in the HTML and save the PDF again.
All links are clickable, in both the HTML and the PDF. Slide 15 links to the team
issues sheet (Google Sheets). Team members need edit access to that sheet to add
their tab, so share it with them in Google Sheets (Share > Editor).

## Message to send with it

> Hi all,
>
> We now have **CAD to Revit**, an app that reads a job's structural CAD drawings
> and builds the Revit model, showing where every size came from. The attached guide
> explains how to use it.
>
> To start: on the office network, open **http://DESKTOP-1B6U4I7:8770/** in Chrome or
> Edge, type your office e-mail, and press **"Set or reset my password"**. A link
> arrives by e-mail (check Spam); it works for 30 minutes. Choose a password, then
> sign in.
>
> Try **"Try a sample"** first to get a feel for it.
>
> Found an issue? Log it in the team issues sheet:
> https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1VJh6xAU9j_JeeLb4shm4b3iZGgynWa3-mIllMAJtcms/edit?gid=0#gid=0
> Make your own tab named after you (the + at the bottom left), and write one issue per
> row: the date, the project and floor, the mark (e.g. B47), what you see and what you
> expected, and a screenshot with the place circled.
