Our side of the work

A Revit template set up once, then reused on every floor

Column family

One rectangular concrete column, types named C-3 230x1200

Beam family

One concrete beam, types B 230x600 through to GB 900x800

Floor types

125, 150, 175 and 200, with sunk zones as level offsets

Wall type

230 structural wall for lift walls LW-1 to LW-4 and the pardi walls

Materials

M30 for beams and slabs, M40 for girders, as two separate materials

Shared parameters

Mark, Top Steel, Bottom Steel, Stirrups, Extra Top, Remark, Concrete Grade

With the parameters carrying the steel, the beam and column schedules come out of the model instead of being drawn by hand, and they can be checked line by line against the GFC sheet.
