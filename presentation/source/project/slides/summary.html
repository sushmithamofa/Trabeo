The answer, up front

The drawings are close. Three things stand in the way.

01

The DWG is already machine-readable

Columns, beams and girders sit on their own layers, and 121 beam tags, 48 column labels and 65 slab tags are real text a script can read.

02

Three gaps block the script

No closed slab outlines, no grid or shared base point, and beam lines drawn on the beam edge instead of its centreline.

03

The fix is a drawing standard

Not new software. A layer list, seven drawing rules and a submission checklist, agreed once and applied to every floor.
