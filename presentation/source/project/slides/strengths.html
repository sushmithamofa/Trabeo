Layers are already split

Columns are closed shapes

Most are closed rectangles, which give a centre point, a size and a rotation directly.

Beams sit at one width

113 beams are 230 wide multilines, so their lines can be pulled out as they are.

Tags are real text

121 beam tags, 48 column labels and 65 slab tags, all readable rather than exploded.

Sizes agree with the schedule

38 of the 48 column labels match the scheduled size exactly, with no interpretation.

The schedules are complete

Beams, GB-1 to GB-6, C-1 to C-32, FC-1 to FC-6 and S-1 to S-10, with concrete grade.
