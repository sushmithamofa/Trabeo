The ask

Eight things with every floor, every issue

01

The DWG, one floor per file, cleaned, flattened and set out from the agreed base point

02

Column schedule as Excel, one row per mark, numbers only

03

Beam and girder schedule as Excel, with every depth filled in

04

Slab schedule as Excel, with thickness and sunk depth per panel

05

A level table from plinth to terrace, with beam top levels

06

Sections through the sunk areas, pool, stairs and lift

07

The grid, or written confirmation that we use the architectural grid

08

A revision list naming what changed since the previous issue

The standard carries a 12-item checklist to tick before each issue

19

Item 8 is the quiet win. A revision list turns each reissue into a targeted re-run instead of a full rebuild, which is where most of the saving actually comes from.
